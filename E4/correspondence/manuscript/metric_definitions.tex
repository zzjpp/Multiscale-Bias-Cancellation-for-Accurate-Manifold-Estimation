\paragraph{Correspondence and geometric errors.}
The same stored reference points $y_{1,j}\in\widehat{\mathcal M}_{h_1}$ are used for all four methods. Put $x_j=\pi_{\mathcal M}(y_{1,j})$ and let $X_{h_2}(x_j)$ denote the intersection of the true normal fibre at $x_j$ with the selected $h_2$-scale STSP branch. For correspondence method $A$ we report
\[
E_{\mathrm{match}}^{A}=\max_j\|Z_{h_2,j}^{A}-X_{h_2}(x_j)\|_2,
\qquad
E_{\mathrm{base}}^{A}=\max_j\|\pi_{\mathcal M}(Z_{h_2,j}^{A})-x_j\|_2.
\]
The true geometry in these definitions is used for evaluation and Oracle, not as an input to continuous CP. The extrapolated point is $\widehat z_j^{A}=w_1y_{1,j}+w_2Z_{h_2,j}^{A}$, and
\[
E_\infty^{A}=\max_j d(\widehat z_j^{A},\mathcal M),
\qquad
E_{\mathrm{avg}}^{A}=\frac1{N_q}\sum_{j=1}^{N_q}d(\widehat z_j^{A},\mathcal M).
\]
The original exact-coefficient STSP weights are retained. They are $(2.7021492127495965,-1.7021492127495965)$ in the finite-sample experiment with $(h_1,h_2)=(2\sigma,4\sigma)$, and $(1.8852837485845062,-0.8852837485845062)$ in the population scan with $(h_1,h_2)=(3\sigma,6\sigma)$. In the latter scan the common reference points are constructed on the true normal charts, so their base points are the fixed original curve queries. Finite-sample summaries are medians and interquartile ranges over ten original parents; population maxima are over the fixed 32-query set, not a continuous supremum over the entire curve.
