# Final-paper scope and limitations

This release targets only `arxiv_final.pdf`, identified by SHA-256 in `PAPER_REFERENCE.json`. Appendix figure numbering is E.1–E.18. Historical figure labels, source paths and original plans remain where required to authenticate immutable numerical evidence; they are not the current figure map.

E2 population overlays were withdrawn and are absent. E3 uses six equal reverse reference counts of 262144 and YY radii (0.4,1,1.6). E4 is local continuous CP with geometry-assisted common-query initialization, not a globally certified empirical nearest-point solver. The previously absent E5 finite packets have been recovered byte-for-byte and are distributed with reaggregation checks; original pilot-selection/conditioning caveats remain disclosed. Numerical failures and partial-query statistics are preserved.
