\caption{Schematic multiscale correspondence and leading-bias cancellation.
(a) Oracle-normal matching defines $X_{h_k}(x)$ along the true normal at $x$;
the weights preserve affine combinations and cancel the leading normal-bias
coefficient. (b) Estimated-normal matching is anchored at the common point
$Z_{h_1}(x)=X_{h_1}(x)$. A second-order normal-direction error,
$\|\widetilde n(x)-n(x)\|=O(h_1^2)$, together with an $O(h_2^2)$ normal
displacement motivates the illustrated fourth-order error estimate for the
extrapolated point,
$\|\widetilde X(x)-x\|=O(h_{\max}^4)$.
Open circles and the open square distinguish oracle and estimated coarse-scale
correspondences, respectively; filled diamonds denote extrapolated points.
The drawing is schematic and not to scale.}
\label{fig:oracle-estimated-normal-correspondence}
