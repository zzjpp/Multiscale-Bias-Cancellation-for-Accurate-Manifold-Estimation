\caption{Schematic leading-order normal-bias decomposition for a curve
$\mathcal M\subset\mathbb R^3$. The dashed arrow from $x$ to
$x+u_{r_1}(x)\in\mathcal M_{r_1}^{\mathrm{YY}}$ denotes the complete normal
displacement. The three colored arrows form a head-to-tail chain of the
scale-weighted leading components in the directions labeled $P_0H$,
$S_0H$, and $b_\perp$; scalar coefficients are suppressed. The first lies
in $\operatorname{Ran}P_0=\operatorname{span}\{\nu_0\}$, whereas the latter
two lie in the one-dimensional space $\operatorname{Ran}S_0$ and are
therefore collinear. The thin gray $\nu_0$ direction marker is translated
slightly in parallel for legibility; it indicates the direction of
$\operatorname{Ran}P_0$. An $O(\sigma^4)$ remainder is not drawn separately,
so the colored chain is not asserted to sum exactly to $u_{r_1}(x)$.
The gray parallelogram represents
the affine true normal plane $x+N_x\mathcal M$. The drawing is an oblique
view and is not to scale.}
\label{fig:yy-normal-decomposition}
