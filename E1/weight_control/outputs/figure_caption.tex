\caption{Population maximum errors under a controlled STSP weight comparison. In the legend, Successive tangent-space projection (STSP), Multiscale successive tangent-space projection (MS-STSP), Squared extrapolation (SQ), and Geometric bias cancellation (GBC) denote Single, Original MS-STSP, Naive, and GBC-STSP (the proposed method), respectively. All three controlled estimators use exactly the same archived smooth-kernel STSP fits and estimated-normal-fiber correspondence at $h_1=3\sigma$ and $h_2=6\sigma$. Single is the matched coarse-scale point $Z_{h_2}$; Naive uses $(4/3,-1/3)$; Proposed uses the exact noise-dependent STSP bias coefficients. Original MS-STSP is the previously computed population adaptation with a hard-ball kernel, its own fixed-reference iteration and radii $\{3,4.5,6\}\sigma$; its measurements are reused without refitting. Axes are logarithmic, with noise level decreasing from left to right. The four unboxed slopes $p_{\mathrm{STSP}}$, $p_{\mathrm{MS\!-\!STSP}}$, $p_{\mathrm{sq}}$, and $p_{\mathrm{GBC\!-\!STSP}}$ are fitted over all seven displayed noise levels and correspond to the four legend entries, respectively. Errors use the original E1 queries: one canonical query on each homogeneous example and 128 fixed meridional queries on the corrugated torus. The plotted maximum is over these finite query sets, not a certified continuous supremum.}
